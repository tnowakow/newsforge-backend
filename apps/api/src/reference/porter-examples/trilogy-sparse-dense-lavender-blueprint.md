# Trilogy Sparse Dense-Lavender Blueprint

Phase 1 target for the Trilogy June/August packet discussed on 2026-08-14.

## Packet Signature

- 10 source modules
- 7 uploaded photos
- 7 referenced photo pairs
- 10 dated schedule/social rows
- Roughly 300 source words
- Many micro-briefs
- No real inner-spread birthday list

## Porter References Used

- Primary: `example-11` (`dense-lavender-grid`) — low-word, photo-led dense grid with many small photos and a lavender/spotlight rail.
- Secondary: `example-17` (`dense-lavender-grid`) — summer outings, dated rows, dense grid rhythm, and footer/anchor behavior.
- Secondary: `example-05` (`dense-lavender-grid`) — dense grid, lavender spotlight rail, navy/footer anchoring.

## Composition Decisions

- Treat Happy Hours, Socials, and Brunch as one schedule rail made from compact stacked list modules.
- Keep Executive Director as a cream anchor, but size it to copy rather than stretching it into dead space.
- Treat Legacy, Chef Circle, and Music to My Ears as photo-led story tiles.
- Treat Resident Council, Out & About, and Intergenerational Fun as a campus brief stack with nearby/caption-led photos.
- Put reclaimed space into a strong photo mass, not taller text panels.
- Preserve every uploaded article and every uploaded photo exactly once.

## Target Zones

- Page 1 left rail: compact schedule/social/brunch stack.
- Page 1 upper middle: Executive Director cream anchor.
- Page 1 right/top and lower middle/right: Legacy, Chef, and Music story/photo tiles.
- Page 2 upper band: campus brief stack plus outing/intergenerational photos.
- Page 2 lower band: large leftover photo mass/rail.
