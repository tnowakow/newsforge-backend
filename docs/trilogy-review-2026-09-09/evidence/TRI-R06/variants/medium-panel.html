PORTER ONE — SUMMER ISSUE
Conservatory mornings become the week's anchor
Coffee service moved into the east conservatory on a trial basis, and the quiet of the space has drawn a steady crowd before nine. Staff are adding a second pour-over station and a small shelf of resident-owned paperbacks.
Watercolor course completes its first cohort
Six residents finished their final studies this month, and a small exhibit runs in the lobby through the end of the season. The class returns in September with a new set of reference materials.
