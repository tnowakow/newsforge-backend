A QUIET SUMMER AT PORTER ONE
The weeks slowed as the courtyard orchard began its late blush. Residents gathered in the east conservatory for long-morning coffee, while the garden path was re-staked after the first warm rain.
A handful of notes from the month: the new reading circle finished its first novel together, two residents completed their watercolor course, and the kitchen began its first seasonal pastry series on Saturday.
From the garden
Lettuce starts were set in the raised beds and the climbing beans were given fresh stakes. Expect the first salad boxes to reach tables by late July.
